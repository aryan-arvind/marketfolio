The Problem Statement — Why Does MarketFolio Exist?

🚨 The Big Trap: Predicting Tomorrow's Stock Price

Most machine learning projects on equities take daily closing prices, pass them through a basic LSTM, and show overlapping lines claiming 98% accuracy. In real academic machine learning and quantitative finance, this is a fundamental fallacy.

1. Weak-Form EMH

Eugene Fama's Nobel Prize-winning Efficient Market Hypothesis proves that price movements follow a Markovian random walk. Today's price already incorporates all historical data. Next-tick direction is white noise.

2. The Lag-1 Illusion

If a neural network simply outputs $\hat{y}_{t+1} = y_t$ (tomorrow's price equals today's price), the lines look identical with a high $R^2$, but the model has zero edge—it is just a 1-day delayed mirror.

3. The Leakage Trap

Using train_test_split(shuffle=True) or fitting scalers across the full dataset leaks future market crashes into training features. The model memorizes the future, creating false high performance.

What MarketFolio Actually Solves

Instead of guessing unpredictable price noise, institutional quant desks care about three real, predictable phenomena:

  • 1. Structural Anomalies & Tail Risk: Has the market entered an unprecedented dislocation (e.g., March 2020 COVID crash) where historical correlations shatter?
  • 2. Volatility Regimes: While price direction is random, volatility clusters heavily (Mandelbrot, 1963). Will the next 10 days experience Low, Normal, or High turbulence?
  • 3. Cross-Market Contagion: When Brent Crude spikes or the US Dollar strengthens, how does that macroeconomic shock transmit across a relational network to hit Reliance Industries?

Deep Anomaly VAE Engine (Generative Latent Modeling)

Syllabus: Generative Models & Variational Inference

The "Financial Lie Detector" Analogy

A standard Autoencoder compresses data into arbitrary numbers and can memorize training noise. A Variational Autoencoder (VAE) forces the latent representations into a continuous probability distribution—a standard bell curve $\mathcal{N}(0, I)$.

We train the VAE on normal market behavior. In normal markets, it reconstructs multivariate indicators with high precision. When an extreme shock occurs (like March 2020), correlations break down and the VAE fails to reconstruct the inputs—the Reconstruction Loss spikes!

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{VAE}} = \underbrace{\frac{1}{D}\sum_{j=1}^{D}(x_j - \hat{x}_j)^2}_{\text{Reconstruction Error (MSE)}} + \beta \cdot \underbrace{D_{\text{KL}}\left(q_\phi(z|x) \,\|\, \mathcal{N}(0, I)\right)}_{\text{Kullback-Leibler Divergence}}$$

The Reparameterization Trick

To sample $z \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$, standard random sampling is non-differentiable (gradients cannot flow through random chance). The Reparameterization Trick isolates the randomness by sampling independent noise $\epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I)$:

$$z = \mu + \sigma \odot \epsilon$$

Now the entire operation is differentiable with respect to $\mu$ and $\sigma$, enabling gradient backpropagation!

Academic Zero-Leakage Safeguard

The anomaly threshold $\tau = 1.60267$ is calibrated at the 95th percentile strictly on historical training data. We never look at test data to decide what constitutes an anomaly.

Volatility Regime Transformer (Multi-Head Self-Attention)

Syllabus: Sequence Modeling & Attention Mechanisms

The "Attentive Financial Historian"

Standard RNNs read time-series step-by-step and suffer from sequential bottlenecks. The Transformer evaluates the entire 20-day historical window simultaneously. If day 18 had a sudden volume spike and day 2 had a currency dip, Self-Attention directly links them with a learned attention weight.

$$\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{Q K^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V$$

Why Volatility Regimes Instead of Stock Prices?

While price direction is random, volatility clusters intensely (Mandelbrot, 1963). We predict the future 10-day volatility regime:

  • Class 0 (Low Volatility): Bottom 33.3% of historical training distribution.
  • Class 1 (Normal Volatility): Middle 33.3%.
  • Class 2 (High Volatility): Top 33.3% (turbulent, high-risk conditions).

The Honest Econometric Finding (Why Persistence Won)

On our held-out test set, the Transformer achieved 62.44% raw accuracy, but had a lower Macro F1 (0.3136) than the Martingale Persistence Baseline (0.3981), which predicts "tomorrow's regime equals today's regime." In real financial markets, volatility regimes persist for long stretches. Acknowledging this benchmark honestly demonstrates academic rigor over synthetic manipulation.

Inter-Market Relational GCN (Non-Euclidean Graph Convolutions)

Syllabus: Graph Neural Networks & Spectral Convolutions

The "Financial Spiderweb"

No asset trades in isolation. Reliance Industries is linked to domestic and global macro factors. We build a 6-node synchronized graph connecting Reliance to NIFTY 50, S&P 500, Brent Crude, USD/INR, and India VIX.

$$H^{(l+1)} = \text{ReLU}\left(\hat{A} H^{(l)} W^{(l)}\right), \quad \text{where } \hat{A} = \tilde{D}^{-1/2} \tilde{A} \tilde{D}^{-1/2}$$

Why Symmetric Degree Normalization Matters

If node $i$ has 5 connections and node $j$ has 1 connection, multiplying by raw adjacency would scale node $i$'s features 5 times larger! Kipf & Welling's degree normalization $\tilde{D}^{-1/2}\tilde{A}\tilde{D}^{-1/2}$ scales neighbor aggregation by their mutual degrees, preventing gradient explosion.

Empirical Result: +20.95% Performance Gain

The GCN achieved a Macro F1 of 0.6520, outperforming single-asset models (0.4425) by +20.95%. This proves mathematically that cross-market network topology provides critical predictive information that isolated asset models miss!

Most Probable Viva Questions & Bulletproof Answers

Q1 Why did you use a VAE instead of a regular Autoencoder?
A standard deterministic Autoencoder maps inputs to fixed discrete points in latent space without continuity constraints, making it prone to overfitting and memorizing noise. A Variational Autoencoder forces latent representations into a continuous probability distribution $\mathcal{N}(0, I)$ via the KL Divergence loss term. This creates a smooth, probabilistic representation of normal market covariance, making reconstruction error far more reliable for flagging true structural dislocations.
Q2 What is the Reparameterization Trick, and why is it needed?
In a VAE, we need to sample a latent vector $z$ from $\mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$. But random sampling is a non-differentiable operation—gradients cannot backpropagate through random chance! The Reparameterization Trick factors out randomness by sampling independent noise $\epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I)$ and computing:
$$z = \mu + \sigma \odot \epsilon$$
This operation is completely differentiable with respect to $\mu$ and $\sigma$, allowing standard gradient descent to train the encoder.
Q3 Did you shuffle your time-series data when splitting? (Trap Question)
No, absolutely not, Mam. In time-series machine learning, shuffling is strictly forbidden because it introduces lookahead bias—the model trains on future data to predict the past. We used a strict chronological partition: 70% Train (2008–2020), 15% Validation (2020–2022), and 15% Held-Out Test (2022–2026). Furthermore, our feature scaler was fit exclusively on the training partition and frozen before transforming the test split.
Q4 What is an Embargo Gap and why did you implement it?
Our target is realized volatility over the next $H=10$ trading days. For a sample at the end of the training set, its target label uses prices extending 10 days into the future. If the test set starts immediately on the next day, the training target overlaps with test set features! We enforce an $H=10$-day Embargo Gap, purging those 10 transition days to guarantee zero lookahead bias.
Q5 Why did your Transformer have a lower Macro F1 than the Persistence baseline?
In financial econometrics, volatility exhibits intense clustering and autoregressive persistence (Mandelbrot, 1963). If today is in a high-volatility regime, tomorrow has an overwhelming probability of remaining high. Because markets spend extended periods in a single regime, the naive Persistence baseline ('predict tomorrow's regime equals today's regime') is very difficult to beat out-of-sample. Rather than faking synthetic numbers, reporting this real econometric finding demonstrates true academic honesty and respects the Efficient Market Hypothesis.
Q6 How did you construct the Adjacency Matrix in the GCN? Why is degree normalization needed?
We compute the pairwise Pearson correlation matrix $\rho_{ij}$ of daily log-returns strictly on the training partition. We apply a threshold $\tau = 0.15$ to eliminate noise, and add self-loops $\tilde{A} = A + I_N$ so each asset retains its own features. We then apply Kipf & Welling's Symmetric Degree Normalization: $\hat{A} = \tilde{D}^{-1/2}\tilde{A}\tilde{D}^{-1/2}$. Without degree normalization, nodes with many connections would scale feature vectors up by their degree during message passing, causing gradient explosion and unstable training.
Q7 What hardware did you train this on?
All models were trained natively on the host multi-threaded CPU using PyTorch 2.5 (torch.cuda.is_available() == False). The dataset contains 4,362 historical trading days (2008 to 2026). Because we designed parameter-efficient architectures ($d_{\text{model}}=32$ for the Transformer, $d_z=4$ for the VAE), the entire pipeline trains in under 5 minutes without needing expensive GPU hardware.

MLOps & Research Governance

Why Trello in a Machine Learning Project?

In quantitative finance and modern MLOps, deploying machine learning models without formal task tracking and research governance is considered high-risk engineering. When managing multiple models (VAE, Transformer, GCN), researchers must track hypotheses, blockers, and zero-leakage validation evidence.

Decoupled 3-State Architecture

A common software design flaw is tightly coupling external third-party APIs into analytical engines: if the third-party API goes down, the entire app crashes! In MarketFolio, the Trello tracker is completely decoupled using a 3-state enum (TrackerSyncMode):

Tracker Mode Trigger Condition System Behavior
LIVE_SYNCHRONIZED Trello API key, token, and board ID are configured and valid. Connects to live Trello REST API via HTTPS. Fetches live cards, allows creating cards and moving lists.
API_CONNECTION_FAILED Credentials present, but internet down or API returns 401/404. Displays an explicit error banner in the UI: "Trello API connection failed. Reverting to local audit roadmap." Never fakes synchronization!
LOCAL_ROADMAP_ONLY No credentials provided (default local academic mode). Displays the curated Local Research Audit Roadmap with 5 quantitative audit stages. Completely offline, zero ML latency.

The 5 Standardized Audit Lists

  1. 1. Planned / Unverified Specification: Models or features proposed but not yet implemented.
  2. 2. In Progress: Features currently under active development.
  3. 3. Blocked / Technical Debt: Tasks delayed by dependencies or computational constraints.
  4. 4. Validation & Leakage Check: Critical audit checklist verifying zero-leakage, stationarity checks, and scaler isolation.
  5. 5. Completed & Verified: Models that have passed the full 23-test suite and real-data training runs.
